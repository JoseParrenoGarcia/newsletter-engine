# Post Type: Management

For posts about leadership, team dynamics, career transitions, EQ, feedback, meetings, recognition, and org design. These are Jose's most personal posts — grounded in lived experience and written for data scientists navigating people management.

The universal rules in `voice.md` and `templates/post_template.md` apply to this post type. The following rules extend or override them specifically for management posts.

---

## Reflect prompts

**Optional mid-section device.** A single question or short set of questions inviting the reader to apply the concept to their own situation. More common in reflective posts than tactical ones.

> *"Take a moment to reflect: when was the last time your manager gave you feedback without being asked? How did that feel?"*

---

## Named blockquotes

Blockquotes use named external authority only — Goleman, Kahneman, Camille Fournier, and similar. Never anonymous quotes. Always attributed on the same line or the line immediately following.

- Do: `> "Quote." — Daniel Goleman, *Emotional Intelligence*`
- Avoid: `> "Great leaders listen first."` (no attribution)
