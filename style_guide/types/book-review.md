# Post Type: Book Review

For posts reviewing books recommended to data scientists, technical leaders, and people managers. Jose's book reviews are not summaries — they are curated lessons extracted from personal reading, framed as a recommendation from practitioner to peer.

The universal rules in `voice.md` and `templates/post_template.md` apply to this post type. The following rules extend or override them specifically for book review posts.

---

## Inline quote format

Book quotes use inline italics + page number: *"Quote text."* [page X]

- Not blockquote format. Blockquote is reserved for standalone attributed quotes from named people cited in the book — not the book's own text.
- Always include the page number — this differentiates a verified quote from a paraphrase.
- Direct pulls only — never paraphrase inside quote markers.

---

## Section anchor structure

Headings follow one of two frameworks:

- **Chapter titles** — book's chapter structure as the anchor
- **Learning #N:** — numbered key lessons extracted ("Learning #1:", "Learning #2:"), used when the book has a non-linear structure or when lessons transcend individual chapters

Sub-sections use question-based or noun-phrase `###` headings:
*"How to grab and keep your audience's attention"*, *"2 styles to avoid"*
